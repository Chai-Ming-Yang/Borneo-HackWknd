# Bijak Pay: Proactive BNPL Financial Awareness System

> **Bridging the gap between impulse and insight.** Bijak Pay is a design-led solution developed to combat the "Buy Now, Pay Later" (BNPL) debt trap through proactive behavioral interventions and automated financial tracking.

## 📑 Project Overview

### The Problem
University students frequently struggle with "present bias," leading to impulsive spending via BNPL services without understanding the long-term impact of fragmented debt.

### The Solution: Bijak Pay
Bijak Pay shifts financial management from reactive tracking to **proactive intervention**.
* **Behavioral Interventions:** Delivers a "nudge" notification immediately after a purchase to explain the real financial burden in plain language.
* **Centralized Tracking:** Aggregates multiple BNPL commitments into a single, intuitive dashboard to reduce cognitive load.
* **AI Analysis:** Simplifies complex repayment terms and hidden fees from invoices into actionable data.

---

## 🧠 Methodology & Design Thinking
We followed a user-centered design methodology to build and test our proposed solution.
* **User Research:** Targeted "The Aspirational Student" persona—tech-savvy young adults who lack structured financial tracking habits.
* **Problem Validation:** Identified that the immediate post-purchase window is the critical "cooling-off" moment for intervention.
* **Visual Design:** Utilized stark alert colors (red) for high-interest obligations and neutral tones for informational inputs to guide user behavior.
