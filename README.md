# Bijak Pay: Proactive BNPL Financial Awareness System

> **Bridging the gap between impulse and insight.** Bijak Pay is an AI-driven solution designed to combat the "Buy Now, Pay Later" (BNPL) debt trap through real-time behavioral interventions and automated financial tracking.

## 📑 Project Overview

### The Problem
While BNPL services offer convenience, they often lead to **fragmented debt** and **opaque obligations**. [cite_start]Users, particularly university students, struggle with "present bias"—making impulsive purchases without understanding the long-term impact of hidden fees and compounding interest[cite: 7, 10, 15].

### Our Solution: Bijak Pay
Bijak Pay shifts financial management from reactive tracking to **proactive intervention**.
* [cite_start]**Centralized Dashboard:** Aggregates all active BNPL debts and calculates precise upcoming payment totals to reduce cognitive load[cite: 18, 127].
* [cite_start]**AI Behavioral "Nudges":** Delivers a real-time notification immediately after a purchase to explain the "true price" of the installment plan in plain language[cite: 19, 114].
* [cite_start]**Automated Invoice Analysis:** Uses AI to extract repayment schedules and hidden fees from uploaded receipts, transforming raw data into meaningful information[cite: 20, 136].

---

## 🛠️ Key Features

### 1. Centralized Financial Tracking
[cite_start]Instead of monitoring multiple apps, users see a unified overview of total outstanding debt and monthly repayment deadlines[cite: 125, 126].

### 2. The Behavioral Nudge (Post-Purchase Intervention)
[cite_start]Intercepts the user during the critical "cooling-off" window[cite: 97, 98].
* [cite_start]**Visual Impact:** Updates the dashboard immediately to show reduced future spending power[cite: 197, 258].
* [cite_start]**Psychological Framing:** Uses strategic color psychology (stark alert tones) to highlight high-interest obligations[cite: 117].

### 3. AI-Assisted Invoice Parsing
[cite_start]Simulates the extraction of interest rates and repayment durations from e-commerce invoices, automating what is traditionally a manual, complex process[cite: 133, 134].
