# Bijak Pay: Proactive BNPL Financial Awareness System

> **Bridging the gap between impulse and insight.** Bijak Pay is a design-led solution developed to combat the "Buy Now, Pay Later" (BNPL) debt trap through proactive behavioral interventions and automated financial tracking.

## 📑 Project Overview

### The Problem
[cite_start]University students frequently struggle with "present bias," leading to impulsive spending via BNPL services without understanding the long-term impact of fragmented debt[cite: 7, 15, 79].

### The Solution: Bijak Pay
Bijak Pay shifts financial management from reactive tracking to **proactive intervention**.
* [cite_start]**Behavioral Interventions:** Delivers a "nudge" notification immediately after a purchase to explain the real financial burden in plain language[cite: 19, 114].
* [cite_start]**Centralized Tracking:** Aggregates multiple BNPL commitments into a single, intuitive dashboard to reduce cognitive load[cite: 18, 127].
* [cite_start]**AI Analysis:** Simplifies complex repayment terms and hidden fees from invoices into actionable data[cite: 20, 136].

---

## 🧠 Methodology & Design Thinking
[cite_start]We followed a user-centered design approach, moving through Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test stages[cite: 71, 75].
* [cite_start]**User Research:** Targeted "The Aspirational Student" persona—tech-savvy young adults who lack structured financial tracking habits[cite: 93].
* [cite_start]**Problem Validation:** Identified that the immediate post-purchase window is the critical "cooling-off" moment for intervention[cite: 97, 98].
